Common questions

What do I need to use Lamp Quest?

An iPhone running iOS 17 or later for your child, and your child added as a child in your Apple Family Sharing group.

How do I set it up on my child’s iPhone?
  1. Install Lamp Quest on your child’s iPhone and open it.
  2. Tap to get started and approve Screen Time access with your own Apple Account password when asked.
  3. Choose the apps to limit, the daily free minutes, the minutes each quest earns and how many quests count per day.
  4. Allow notifications so the “Start a quest” button can open a quest.
How does my child earn more time?

When limited apps lock, your child opens a quest: a short Bible story, a verse and three questions. Answering every question correctly unlocks the minutes you chose (15 or more). Once they reach your daily quest limit, the apps stay locked until tomorrow.

What happens if my child gets an answer wrong?

There’s a short pause of a few seconds, then they can try again. The pause encourages reading the story instead of guessing.

I tapped “Start a quest” on a locked app and nothing opened.

Apple doesn’t let the lock screen open another app directly, so Lamp Quest sends a notification instead. Tap the “Your quest is ready!” notification to open the quest. If you don’t see it, check Settings › Notifications › Lamp Quest and make sure notifications are allowed.

When does the daily time reset?

Each day at midnight. The free minutes and the quest count start over, and locked apps unlock.

Which Bible translation do you use?

Verses are from the King James Version. Stories are retold in simple words for kids.

How do I stop using Lamp Quest?

Delete the app from your child’s iPhone. Because Lamp Quest is set up through Screen Time with Family Sharing, removing it may ask for your approval. Once it’s gone, the limits it set are lifted and its data on the device is deleted.

Does Lamp Quest collect my child’s data?

No. There are no accounts, ads or tracking, and settings stay on the device. See our privacy policy for details.
