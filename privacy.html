Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

The short version

  • Lamp Quest does not collect, sell or share personal information from children or parents.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party trackers in the app.
  • Your settings and your child’s quest progress stay on your child’s iPhone.
  • Apple’s Screen Time keeps your child’s app choices private. Lamp Quest cannot see which apps your child uses.

Who we are

Lamp Quest is a screen time app for iPhone that lets parents limit chosen apps and lets children earn more time by completing short Bible quests. Lamp Quest is operated by Peerless. In this policy, “Lamp Quest,” “we” and “us” mean Peerless, the Lamp Quest app and this website. You can reach us at support@lampquestapp.com.

Information that stays on the device

To work, Lamp Quest saves a small amount of information on your child’s iPhone:

  • The limits you set: daily free minutes, minutes earned per quest and quests allowed per day.
  • Today’s progress: how many quests have been completed and whether apps are currently locked.

This information is stored only on the device, inside the app’s private storage. It is not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting Lamp Quest removes it.

Screen Time and the apps your child uses

Lamp Quest uses Apple’s Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity). When you choose apps to limit, Apple gives Lamp Quest private tokens that stand in for those apps. Lamp Quest cannot see the names of the apps you pick, the websites your child visits or how your child uses other apps. Apple tracks screen time on the device and tells Lamp Quest only when the daily allowance is used up. None of this is sent to us.

Notifications

When your child taps “Start a quest” on a locked app, Lamp Quest shows a notification that opens the quest. These are local notifications created on the device. They do not go through our servers.

Children’s privacy

Lamp Quest is made for children under 13 to use under a parent’s supervision, and we take the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) seriously. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app has no sign-up, chat, sharing, photos, location or contact features, and children cannot send information to us through it.

If we ever add a feature that would collect personal information from children, we will update this policy first and get verifiable parental consent before any collection begins.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to answer you and keep it only as long as needed to help you. Our email is handled by Google (Gmail), whose own privacy policy applies to how it stores mail. Please don’t include your child’s personal information in messages to us.

This website

lampquestapp.com does not use cookies, analytics or third-party trackers, and it loads no outside fonts or scripts. Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, for security and to keep the site running.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell or rent personal information.
  • We don’t show ads or use advertising or tracking tools.
  • We don’t use Screen Time information for any purpose other than limiting and unlocking the apps you chose.

Your choices and rights

Because Lamp Quest keeps your family’s information on your child’s device, you are always in control of it. You can change limits at any time in the app, and deleting the app deletes its data. If you have emailed us and want that correspondence deleted, or you have any question about your child’s information, write to support@lampquestapp.com and we will respond.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Lamp Quest handles information, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect. If a change affects children’s information, we will get parental consent where the law requires it.
