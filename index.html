How it works

Set it up once on your child’s iPhone. Lamp Quest handles the rest.

You choose the apps

Pick the games, video and social apps to limit, then set how many free minutes your child gets each day.

Time’s up, apps lock

When the daily time is used, those apps show a friendly Lamp Quest screen instead. Everything else keeps working.

A quest earns more

Your child reads a short Bible story, learns a verse and answers three questions to unlock more time (15 minutes or more, your choice). You decide how many quests count each day.

Made for parents who want more than a timer

Limits are easy. Lamp Quest turns the moment screen time ends into a few minutes with God’s Word.

You stay in charge

Lamp Quest uses Apple’s Screen Time with Family Sharing, so setup needs your approval. You choose the apps, the daily allowance and how much time a quest earns.

Scripture at the center

Each quest retells a Bible story in kid-friendly words and pairs it with a King James Version verse, from David and Goliath to Jesus feeding the 5,000.

Reading, not guessing

Every answer has to be right to earn time. A wrong answer starts a short pause before the next try, so kids slow down and read the story.

Private by design

No ads, no accounts, no tracking. Apple keeps your child’s app choices on the device; Lamp Quest never sees which apps they use. Read our privacy policy.

What you’ll need: an iPhone running iOS 17 or later for your child, and your child added to your Apple Family Sharing group.
